## 中文稿

**数据与协议。** 本文使用的三个基准数据集均为公开数据集：OGBG-PPA、CIFAR10 超像素与 COLLAB。我们采用其标准公开划分，在相互匹配的揭示预算下报告准确率（%）：节点预算 5/10/15%（COLLAB 为 10/15/20%），逐图预算为 $B=\max(1,\lfloor r\cdot|V|\rfloor)$（不含种子）。种子节点由图索引经确定性公式 $(\mathit{idx}\times 31+\mathit{seed}_i\times 17+7)\bmod|V|$ 产生；主结果为每图 4 个确定性种子的平均。评估完全确定：策略以 argmax 选择前沿节点、分数并列取更小节点 id，因此本文每个数字都可精确复现。完整的可见性与预算记账规则在 §3 给出。

超参和训练细节

|                      | ppa | cifar10superpixels | collab |
| :------------------: | :-: | ------------------ | ------ |
|  num of GINE layer  |  2  | 2                  | 2      |
|      hidden dim      | 256 | 128                | 128    |
|      bc epochs      | 10 | 10                 | 10     |
| rl epochs(soft both) | 10 | 10                 | 10     |
|  rl epochs(reader)  |  5  | 5                  | 5      |
|        λ\_ev        |  1  | 1                  | 1      |

**发布。** 上述全部超参数与架构均内嵌于配置文件与 checkpoint。录用后，我们将发布完整源代码、教师轨迹生成与过滤管线、训练好的 checkpoint，以及足以复现本文全部表格与图形的评估脚本。

## English Version

**Data and protocol.** All three benchmarks used in this paper are publicly available: OGBG-PPA, CIFAR10 superpixels, and COLLAB. We adopt their standard public splits and report accuracy (%) at matched reveal budgets of 5/10/15% of nodes (10/15/20% for COLLAB), where the per-graph budget is B = max(1, floor(r·|V|)) excluding the seed. The seed node is (idx×31 + seed_i×17 + 7) mod |V|, a deterministic function of the graph index; the main results average over four deterministic seeds per graph. Evaluation is deterministic: the policy selects frontier nodes by argmax with ties broken by the smaller node id, so every number in this paper can be regenerated exactly. The full visibility and budget accounting rules are specified in Section 3.

**Hyperparameters and training details.** Teacher trajectories are generated by gradient saliency, information gain, structural heuristics, and their hybrids, filtered by the frozen classifier's correctness on the final revealed subgraph and class-balanced; all other hyperparameters are listed below.

|                                |          PPA          |  CIFAR10 superpixels  |        COLLAB        |
| :-----------------------------: | :-------------------: | :-------------------: | :-------------------: |
|       num of GINE layers       |           2           |           2           |           2           |
|           hidden dim           |          256          |          128          |          128          |
|        BC optimizer / lr        |      Adam, 1e-3      |      Adam, 1e-3      |      Adam, 1e-3      |
|          BC batch size          |          64          |          64          |          64          |
|            BC epochs            |          10          |          10          |          10          |
|  RL algorithm / optimizer / lr  | REINFORCE, Adam, 1e-4 | REINFORCE, Adam, 1e-4 | REINFORCE, Adam, 1e-4 |
| RL batch size (graphs × seeds) |        32 × 4        |        32 × 4        |        32 × 4        |
|      RL epochs (soft both)      |          10          |          10          |          10          |
|       RL epochs (reader)       |           5           |           5           |           5           |
|       rollout temperature       |          0.2          |          0.2          |          0.2          |

|    advantage standardization    |      batch-level      |      batch-level      |      batch-level      |
|              λ_ev              |           1           |           1           |           1           |

**Release.** All hyperparameters and architectures above are embedded in the configuration files and checkpoints. Upon acceptance, we will release the full source code, the teacher trajectory generation and filtering pipeline, trained checkpoints, and evaluation scripts sufficient to reproduce every table and figure in this paper.
