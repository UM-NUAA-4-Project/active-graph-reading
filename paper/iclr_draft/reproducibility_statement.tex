\section*{Reproducibility Statement}

\textbf{Data and protocol.} All three benchmarks are publicly available: OGBG-PPA \refadd{ogbg}, CIFAR10 superpixels \refadd{cifar10}, and COLLAB \refadd{tudataset}. We adopt their standard public splits and report accuracy (\%) at matched reveal budgets of 5/10/15\% of nodes (10/15/20\% for COLLAB), where the per-graph budget is $B=\max(1,\lfloor r\cdot|V|\rfloor)$ excluding the seed. The seed node is $(\mathit{idx}\times 31+\mathit{seed}_i\times 17+7)\bmod|V|$, a deterministic function of the graph index; the main results average over four deterministic seeds per graph. Evaluation is deterministic: the policy selects frontier nodes by argmax with ties broken by the smaller node id, so every number in this paper can be regenerated exactly. The full visibility and budget accounting rules are specified in \S\ref{subsec:protocol}.

\textbf{Hyperparameters and training details.} Teacher trajectories are generated by gradient saliency, information gain, structural heuristics, and their hybrids, filtered by the frozen classifier's correctness on the final revealed subgraph and class-balanced; all other hyperparameters are listed below.
