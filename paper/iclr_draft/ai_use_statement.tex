\section*{AI Use Statement}

In accordance with the ICLR 2027 policy on AI disclosure, we describe how large language model (LLM) based tools were used in this work. LLM assistants, including conversational LLMs and LLM-based coding agents, were used in four ways. First, for retrieval and discovery: locating and cross-checking related work, including the datasets and baselines compared in this paper. Second, for research ideation and execution: coding assistants helped implement, debug, and refactor parts of the experimental infrastructure, such as the teacher trajectory generation, training, and evaluation pipelines; all experiments were designed, launched, and verified by the authors. Third, for drafting sections: LLMs produced draft prose for parts of the manuscript, which the authors then rewrote and restructured. Fourth, to aid and polish writing: language editing of the final text.

The authors reviewed and verified all content of this submission, including every claim, reported number, citation, and line of code. No LLM is an author of this work, no LLM conducted or supervised experiments on its own, and no LLM-generated synthetic data is used in any experiment. The authors take full responsibility for the entire submission.
